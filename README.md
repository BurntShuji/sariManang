# Sari-Manang

a standalone mobile application designed to improve operational efficiency and increase long-term business success 
for novice sari-sari store owners who often lack formal business knowledge and experience. 

## Features

To prevent business failure caused by reliance on instinct, the app addresses five key operational needs through a series of integrated tools: 
1) A Pricing Aid to calculate mark-ups based on product cost and target margins;
2) Sales Tracking with estimations on product performance highlights;
3) Utang/Credit Tracker with active payment reminders;
4) Inventory System that offers low-stock and expiration monitoring alerts;
5) A centralized Dashboard that displays the summary of all key data (e.g., total sales of the day, utang reminders, stock alerts, etc.).
